Overview

There is no account creation, login, or password for WARDR. We do not operate a database of user profiles. The photos of your clothes, the outfits you generate, your style profile, and your packing lists are stored locally in the App's storage on your device. We never see them unless you explicitly contact us (for example, by emailing a screenshot for support).

Data that stays on your device

The App stores the following only in local app storage on your device:

  • Photos and details of clothing items you scan (category, color, pattern, season)
  • Outfits you generate or save, and which items you've marked as worn
  • Your style profile / archetype and personal preferences (e.g. favorite colors)
  • Packing lists you create for trips
  • App settings such as your chosen language

Clothing recognition (identifying category, color, pattern, and season from a photo) runs using on-device AI models — the photo does not need to leave your phone for this to work, and the App is fully usable offline.

When the App uses the network

Some optional features require an internet connection and send limited data to our backend, which relays the request to Google's Gemini API:

FeatureWhat is sentWhy
Enhanced clothing scan (fallback) The photo of the item Used only if the on-device model can't confidently classify an item and you're online; the photo is processed to return a result and is not retained by us afterward.
Trending styles / fashion search Your search text, or the current season To fetch up-to-date trend and style results via web search grounding.
Weather-based outfit suggestions Your approximate location (if you grant location permission) and your wardrobe item list To fetch current weather for your area and suggest a weather-appropriate outfit from clothes you already own.

These requests are made only when you actively use the corresponding feature while online. We do not track your location in the background, and none of this is required for the App's core scanning, wardrobe, or outfit-generation functionality.

Third-party services

WARDR uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — shows banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads (watching one grants extra scans). AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API / Search Grounding — powers the optional online features described above. See Google AI terms.
  • Meta (Facebook SDK) — collects standard, non-identifying app-event data (such as app opens and feature usage) for basic analytics. WARDR does not use Facebook Login and does not require a Facebook account. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple / Google device backup (iCloud or Android/Google backup) — if enabled on your device at the OS level, your device's own backup will include the App's local storage like any other app data, so your wardrobe can be restored if you switch devices. This is managed entirely by Apple or Google, not by us.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Permissions the App may request

  • Camera — to photograph clothing items for your digital wardrobe.
  • Photo Library — to import existing photos of clothing, and to save generated outfit images if you choose to.
  • Location (approximate) — optional, used only for weather-based outfit suggestions. The App works fully without it.

You can deny or later revoke any of these in your device's system settings; the App will simply disable the related feature.

Backups & switching devices

WARDR does not run its own cloud sync or require a subscription to back up your closet. Instead, because your data lives in normal app storage, it is automatically included whenever your phone performs its own backup (iCloud on iOS, or your Google/Android account backup on Android) — nothing to set up inside the App.

Your choices & rights

Because WARDR keeps your data on your device rather than in an account we control:

  • Access / export — everything the App knows about your wardrobe is visible directly in the App.
  • Deletion — delete individual items or outfits in the App, or remove all local data at once by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage/data in your device settings.
  • Ads personalization — manage ad tracking and personalization through your device's system-level privacy settings (e.g. "Limit Ad Tracking" / "Opt out of Ads Personalization").

If you are in the EU/UK, California, or another region with data-protection laws and have questions about the limited data described in the "When the App uses the network" section above, contact us at the email below.

Children's privacy

WARDR is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. As the App requires no account, we do not knowingly collect any identifying information from anyone, of any age.
