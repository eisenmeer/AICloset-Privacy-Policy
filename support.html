Help with the WARDR app

Have a question, found a bug, or need something fixed? We read every email.

Email youssefmyh@gmail.com

Do I need to create an account?

No. WARDR works fully without sign-in — open the app and start scanning your closet right away.

Does WARDR work without internet?

Yes. Scanning clothes and generating outfits run with on-device AI, so the core app works offline. A connection is only used for optional extras like trending styles, fashion search, and weather-based suggestions.

How do I delete an item or outfit?

Open the item or outfit inside the app and use the delete option in its details view. Deleted items are removed immediately from your device.

How do I delete all my data?

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage/data from your device's Settings > Apps menu. Since everything is stored locally, this removes it completely. See our Privacy Policy for details on what's stored and where.

Will I lose my wardrobe if I switch phones?

Not if your device's own backup is turned on. WARDR's data is included automatically in your iCloud backup (iOS) or Google/Android device backup (Android) — there's nothing to set up inside the app.

Why do I see ads, and what's the rewarded video for?

WARDR is free and supported by ads. Watching a short rewarded video anytime grants extra scans for that month — it's entirely optional.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

Email us at youssefmyh@gmail.com with your device model, OS version, and what happened — screenshots help a lot.
