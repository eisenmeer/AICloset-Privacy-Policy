01The short version

AICloset works fully offline by default: your wardrobe photos, generated outfits, and style profile are stored only on your device unless you deliberately turn on cloud backup. We don't sell your data to anyone. The app shows ads (via Google AdMob) and lets you optionally sign in with a Google account to back up your closet to the cloud. That's the whole shape of it — the sections below spell out the specifics.

03Photos & camera

When you scan a clothing item, the app uses your device's camera or photo library. In the current version, photo analysis (background removal, category and color detection) happens entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded to us or to any AI service for this. Photos are stored in the app's local storage, and additionally in Google Firebase if you've turned on cloud backup and signed in (see Account & cloud sync).

04Location

The app requests coarse and fine location permission to fetch local weather (via the Open-Meteo weather service) so outfit suggestions can account for the weather where you are. Your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve a forecast and are not paired with your identity or stored by us. Location access is optional — the app works without it, falling back to manually entered weather conditions.

05Account & cloud sync

By default, AICloset stores everything locally on your device and never asks you to create an account. If you choose to subscribe to cloud backup, you can sign in with a Google account. When you do, we create a record tied to your account in Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) containing:

  • Your name, email address, and profile photo, as provided by Google
  • Your wardrobe items, saved outfits, and worn-outfit history

This data is used solely to sync your closet across your own devices. It is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. Firestore security rules restrict every account's data so only that signed-in account can read or write it.

06Advertising

AICloset shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded-video ads) unless you have an active ad-free subscription. AdMob uses your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads, and may use it for ad personalization depending on your Android ad settings. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising ID use at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

08Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud-synced data (if you've signed in) is retained until you delete your account or request deletion — see below.

09Deleting your data

If you've signed in, open Profile → Cloud Account in the app and choose "Delete My Account & Data." This permanently deletes your cloud wardrobe data, your outfit history, and your account itself, and clears local data on that device — immediately and irreversibly. If you'd rather not use the app to do this, email us at the address below and we'll delete it for you within 30 days.

No account, no cloud data. If you've never signed in, there's nothing stored outside your device — uninstalling the app removes everything.

10Children's privacy

AICloset is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll remove it.

11Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

12Contact

Questions about this policy or a data deletion request that isn't covered above: reach out via the support contact listed on the AICloset Play Store listing.
